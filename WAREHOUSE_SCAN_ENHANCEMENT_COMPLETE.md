## 🎓 User Training Quick Guide

### For New Users:

**Step 1: Access the Scanner**
- Go to warehouse dashboard
- Click "Scan Products" (orange button)

**Step 2: Choose Scan Mode**
- **Manual**: Type/paste barcode (good for one-off lookups)
- **Camera**: Point and scan (fastest method)
- **Bulk**: Scan many items in sequence

**Step 3: Camera Setup (First Time)**
- Allow camera permission when prompted
- Position barcode in center frame
- Hold steady for 1-2 seconds
- Wait for auto-detection

**Step 4: Review Results**
- See product details
- Check storage location
- View movement history
- Scan another or return

**Step 5: Export Data (Optional)**
- Click "Export History"
- Download CSV file
- Submit to manager

## 🐛 Known Issues & Workarounds

### Issue 1: Camera Permission Denied
**Workaround**: Use manual input mode or grant permission in browser settings

### Issue 2: Flash Not Working
**Workaround**: Ensure good lighting or use external flashlight

### Issue 3: Zoom Not Available
**Workaround**: Move closer to barcode or use different device

### Issue 4: Scan History Not Saving
**Workaround**: Check browser storage settings, clear cache if needed
