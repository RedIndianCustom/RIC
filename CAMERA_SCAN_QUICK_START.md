# 📷 Camera Scanning - Quick Start Guide

## 🎉 **CAMERA SCANNING IS NOW LIVE!**

Your barcode scanning system now supports **camera-based scanning** in addition to manual input!

---

## 🚀 **Quick Demo (30 seconds)**

### **Step 1: Navigate to Scanner**
```
Dashboard → Scan Barcode
OR
Direct: /scan-barcode
```

### **Step 2: Activate Camera**
1. Click the **"Camera"** tab
2. Browser asks: **"Allow camera access?"**
3. Click **"Allow"**
4. Camera starts automatically! 📷

### **Step 3: Scan a Barcode**
1. Point camera at any barcode
2. Keep it steady in the red box
3. Wait 1-2 seconds
4. **BEEP!** Auto-scanned! ✨

### **Step 4: View Results**
- Warehouse location shown (big green card)
- Product details displayed
- Click "Process Return" if needed
- Or "Scan Another" to continue!

## 💡 **Pro Tips**

### **Scanning Tips:**
1. **Lighting is key!** 💡
   - Bright room = fast scanning
   - Dim room = slower detection
   - Natural daylight = best

2. **Hold steady!** 🤚
   - Keep camera/barcode still for 2-3 seconds
   - Movement = slower detection

3. **Distance matters!** 📏
   - Mobile: 6-12 inches
   - Webcam: 4-8 inches
   - Too close = blurry
   - Too far = can't detect

4. **Use landscape mode on mobile!** 📱
   - Hold phone horizontally
   - Better camera angle
   - Easier to aim

5. **Clean lens = better scans!** 🧼
   - Wipe camera lens
   - Remove smudges
   - Crystal clear = instant detection

---

### **Troubleshooting:**

**Problem:** "Camera Error" message
**Solution:** 
- Click "Allow" when browser asks
- Check Settings → Privacy → Camera
- Close other apps using camera
- Try different browser

**Problem:** Camera shows but won't scan
**Solution:**
- Add more light
- Move closer to barcode
- Hold very steady
- Try different angle
- Use Manual mode instead

**Problem:** Takes too long to scan
**Solution:**
- Improve lighting
- Clean camera lens
- Hold steadier
- Get closer
- Consider USB scanner
