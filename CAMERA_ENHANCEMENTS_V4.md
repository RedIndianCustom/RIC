## 💡 **Usage Tips**

### **For Best Results:**

1. **Optimal Distance:** 6-12 inches
   - Too close = blurry
   - Too far = can't detect
   - Sweet spot = 8-10 inches

2. **Good Lighting:** Bright and even
   - Natural daylight = best
   - Office lighting = good
   - Dim areas = use flash

3. **Steady Hands:** Hold still for 1-2 seconds
   - Movement = slower detection
   - Steady = instant scan

4. **Center Alignment:** Use crosshair guide
   - Align barcode with center
   - Use corner brackets as reference
   - Watch instruction overlay

5. **Use Zoom:** For difficult scans
   - Small barcodes = zoom in
   - Far away = zoom in
   - Large QR codes = zoom out
